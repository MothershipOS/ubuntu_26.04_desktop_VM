name: Ubuntu 26.04 Desktop
summary: Ubuntu's desktop with the phone's GPU and hardware video decoding.

Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, built on the phone from Ubuntu's official cloud image, with its own kernel and Mesa for the
phone's GPU and hardware video decoding. The first start installs the desktop (needs the network, takes a while).
Updating replaces the kernel and keeps your disk. Source: https://github.com/MothershipOS/ubuntu_26.04_desktop_VM
